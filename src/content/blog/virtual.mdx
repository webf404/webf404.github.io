future404 ehf. and 1984 ehf. have signed a contract with Nextcloud GmbH. In collaboration the two companies will be regional representatives for Nextcloud, offering subscriptions to the Virtual office space, technical set up and support.

Nextcloud is a suite of client-server software for creating and using file hosting services. Nextcloud Enterprise offers the quickest and most seamless solutions to the combined need of security and ubiquitous access to data and collaboration technology. The unique, on-premise solutions enable enterprises to regain control over their data and maintain compliance with regulations like GDPR and HIPAA. The most popular self-hosted content collaboration platform combines the convenience and ease of use of consumer-grade solutions like Dropbox and Microsoft 365 with the security and control business needs. With top-rated mobile, web and desktop clients, users can access, sync and share their data, use video chat, access calendars, edit documents or manage tasks within and across organizational borders while the IT team can keep tabs on data access and ensure compliance. All of Nextcloud’s technology is fully open source, avoiding vendor-lock in and deep integration in existing infrastructure like LDAP/Active Directory. The Nextcloud Universal File Access feature makes data available to employees irrespective of where it is stored, be it on a local NAS, a remote FTP, Samba, SharePoint or Amazon S3 store. Powerful collaboration technology is integrated with Nextcloud Talk and Nextcloud Groupware, online office and more, offering seamless real-time communication and chat, collaborative document editing, calendaring and contacts so employees can be productive in one place. Security is Nextcloud customers’​ greatest concerns and our prime advantage over competitors. Nextcloud features a host of unique, innovative security technologies from brute force protection to advanced server side and end-to-end, client side encryption with enterprise-grade key handling and a wide range of security hardenings. The security has been reviewed by trusted third parties and is backed by a $10.000 Security Bug Bounty Program, providing the confidence that data meant to stay private will stay private.
